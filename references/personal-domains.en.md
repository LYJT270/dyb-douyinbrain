# Personal domains: life, work, and learning

[中文](personal-domains.md)

This guide is reached from the [personal knowledge guide](personal-vault.en.md). The three domains are continuing conversation paths, not a questionnaire to finish. New-library setup asks only which domain to start with: life, work, or learning; new libraries enable all three by default. Existing libraries keep their prior collection authorization, and new domains participate only after the user explicitly expands scope. A user can explicitly turn off a domain with `kb module <domain> --off`. Ask one useful core question per turn, check existing material first, and follow from the user's last answer. Pause, stop, decline, or change subject when the user does; a complete basic profile is not a reason to end.

## Life

Choose the most relevant area for the current goal. Phrase questions in original, general language; do not copy third-party question banks, article text, or personal profiles:

- **Background and experience:** Which experiences most shaped how you see things or make choices today?
- **Values, decisions, and motivation:** When two important things conflict, what usually helps you decide which to attend to first?
- **Health, routines, and pace:** What most affects your energy or state during a typical day?
- **Relationships and family:** Which relationships or shared responsibilities usually matter when you make significant plans?
- **Finances and practical constraints:** What limits around time, budget, or responsibilities should a plan account for?
- **Goals and plans:** What are you most trying to move forward now, and what is the next step?

Record only what the user chooses to share; do not fill sensitive or unknown details by guessing. Keep verifiable facts, the user's experiences, and Agent inferences distinct.

## Work

Explore the career, project, business, or creative work most relevant to the user's current goal. Read existing project materials and approved examples first, then ask one question that advances the task, such as current responsibilities, audience, recent outcomes, or resource and time constraints. Keep a project's or account's background, style, and decisions within that work context rather than treating them as universal personal preferences.

## Learning

Follow a continuing sequence: clarify **learning goals and relevant background**, then adapt through **explanation, practice, application, and feedback**. Record only understanding, exercises, applications, and feedback the user has actually shown. Teaching something does not prove that the user has mastered it. Ask one question at a time, focused on a useful next step.

## Across domains and pauses

A clear task takes priority; ordinary work does not automatically become an interview. Within the enabled scope, proactively save clear new facts, changed states or goals, and actual outcomes; briefly mention material changes in the result. After a meaningful stretch, check existing and imported material before occasionally inviting the user to start an unstarted domain. Keep skipped, declined, and paused topics in their current state without repeated prompting.
